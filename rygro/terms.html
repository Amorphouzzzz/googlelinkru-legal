Personal-use terms

A personal monitoring aid.

ryGRO Monitor Alerts is maintained by its owner for use with his private local grow-room monitor. It is not offered here as a public service, subscription or downloadable product.

Authorized use

Connect only a Google account you are entitled to authorize. The application sends monitor alerts to its configured approved recipient using the send-only permission granted on Google's consent screen. Use of Google services remains subject to the applicable Google terms and policies.

What the alerts can and cannot do

Alerts report conditions observed by the monitor and may be delayed by the configured persistence period, delivery retries or service interruptions. The alert worker depends on fresh monitor data, a running PC, internet access, valid Google authorization and Gmail availability. It cannot send an email when those requirements are unavailable.

Email alerts are an additional way to notice a problem. They do not guarantee detection or delivery, provide an emergency-response service, or replace checking the equipment and its own protective features. The email worker itself does not turn equipment on or off.

Owner controls and responsibilities

The owner maintains the PC and equipment, reviews target settings, verifies alert delivery, and reconnects Google access if it expires or is revoked. Email alerts can be disabled in the monitor's Settings page. Google access can also be revoked through the Google Account connections page.

Availability and changes

This personal application has no promised uptime, support response time or delivery schedule. Its owner may modify or discontinue the integration. Changes to data handling should be documented in the privacy policy.

Contact

Contact the app owner through the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
