Email alerts for a private grow-room monitor.

ryGRO Monitor Alerts is a personal application maintained by its owner. It runs on the owner's Windows PC and sends notifications about sensor, equipment and climate-target problems.

Local monitoringChecks the monitor's sensor and equipment status.
Send-only accessUses Google authorization to send email through Gmail.
Personal useSends to the single recipient approved in the application.

What it does

The application detects sustained problems, sends reminders while a problem continues, and reports when a condition has recovered. Alerts may describe an unavailable sensor, an equipment connection problem, or a temperature, humidity or estimated VPD reading outside the configured limits. The owner can also request a test email.

Why it connects to Google

The owner authorizes the Gmail permission gmail.send on Google's consent screen. The application uses this permission to send its alert messages. It does not request permission to read the inbox, search existing mail, manage messages, or access contacts.

Camera images and video are not included in these email alerts. The alert worker does not issue commands to the heater, fan or humidifier.

Keeping control

The owner can turn off email alerts in the monitor's Settings page or revoke the application's Google access. Delivery depends on the PC, internet connection, Google authorization and Gmail service being available.

Read the privacy policy for the information used and stored, and the personal-use terms for the application's limitations.

Contact
