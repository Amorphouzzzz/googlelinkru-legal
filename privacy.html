Privacy Policy — GoogleLinkRU

Effective September 18, 2026

GoogleLinkRU is a personal OAuth application owned and operated by Ryan Uhrich. It connects the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive) to locally running automation software on the owner's personal devices. This policy describes what that means for your data.

What the app accesses

When you authorize GoogleLinkRU, Google's consent screen shows exactly what it requests, and nothing more:

  • Gmail — search and read email, including attachments, only within the authorized account.
  • Google Calendar — read calendars and events; create events when an approved automation needs to.
  • Google Drive — read and download files; create new folders and upload new files into the account's Drive.

The app does not send, delete, or modify email; it does not delete calendar events or send invitations; and it does not change, share, or delete existing Drive files unless the account owner explicitly instructs it to.

Where data is stored

  • Temporary access tokens issued by Google are stored only on the owner's personal computer in its secured credential storage. They are never transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server.
  • Message, calendar, and file contents are not copied or retained by the app. Data stays in your Google account, governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. There are no advertisements, trackers, or analytics in this application.

Revoking access

You can see and revoke this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by visiting Google Cloud Console → Credentials.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app owner through the support email shown on the Google consent screen for GoogleLinkRU.
